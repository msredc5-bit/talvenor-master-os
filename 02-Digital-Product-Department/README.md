## Why Vet Kit

- Vet clinic no-show ~11% (AAHA), average visit ~$200 (AVMA) — one
  clinic has $50k–$150k per year at risk (labeled estimate).
- ~34,296 vet practices in the US.
- Vet = Talvenor's target niche #2. A kit buyer is by definition a pre-qualified
  receptionist prospect (wedge).
- Dental/medical (patient-health) is never a target — this is animal care.
